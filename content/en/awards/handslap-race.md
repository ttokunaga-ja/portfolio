## Recognized presentation

At Human Interface Symposium 2026, the demonstration “A Hand-Based VR Locomotion System for Running as a Horse Avatar” (HandSlapRace, presentation 1T-D16W) received the Outstanding Presentation Award by attendee vote. I co-authored the paper for the recognized demonstration.
